About ABES Engineering College

Established in 2000, ABES Engineering College (ABES EC), Ghaziabad, is one of the leading institutions under Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Technical University (AKTU), Lucknow. Known for its commitment to academic excellence, innovation, and holistic student development, ABES has consistently ranked among the top private engineering colleges in North India.

Our Mission

To create an ambiance for a healthy teaching-learning process that nurtures creativity, inspires innovation, and builds character, preparing students to become responsible global citizens.

Our Vision

To be a leading institution recognized for excellence in education, innovation in research, and contribution to society through sustainable technological and entrepreneurial solutions.

Core Values

  • Integrity and Academic Excellence
  • Innovation and Research-Driven Approach
  • Inclusiveness and Equal Opportunity
  • Commitment to Quality and Sustainability
  • Community Engagement and Social Responsibility

History & Legacy

Since its inception in 2000 under the aegis of the Society of Educational Excellence, ABES Engineering College has grown from a single department to a multidisciplinary institution offering a wide range of undergraduate and postgraduate programs. With a focus on innovation and entrepreneurship, the college has established a strong reputation for producing technically skilled and industry-ready professionals.

Academics & Departments

ABES offers a variety of programs including B.Tech in Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence, Electronics, Mechanical, Civil, Electrical, and related domains, as well as postgraduate programs like MCA and M.Tech. Each department is equipped with advanced laboratories, expert faculty, and modern teaching aids to ensure hands-on and conceptual learning.

Research & Innovation

The college emphasizes research and development through dedicated innovation labs, startup cells, and collaboration with industries. Students and faculty have filed multiple patents and published numerous papers in reputed journals. The incubation center actively supports new ideas and startups, helping young entrepreneurs bring their innovations to life.

Campus & Infrastructure

Spread across 17 acres of lush green land, the campus is equipped with modern facilities including smart classrooms, fully networked computer centers, a central library, innovation labs, seminar halls, hostels, and sports arenas. The environment is designed to encourage creativity, teamwork, and personal growth.

Student Life & Clubs

ABES believes in holistic development through active participation in co-curricular and extracurricular activities. The college hosts several clubs like the Coding Club, Robotics Club, Literary Society, Music & Dance Club, Entrepreneurship Cell, and TechFest Committee. These student-led bodies organize national-level events, hackathons, and cultural festivals that foster leadership and collaboration.

Placements & Industry Connect

ABES maintains a strong record of placements with top recruiters such as Infosys, TCS, Wipro, Accenture, Amazon, and Capgemini. The Training and Placement Cell ensures students receive continuous guidance through aptitude training, mock interviews, and internship opportunities. The highest package for 2024 reached ₹59.91 LPA, showcasing the institute’s industry credibility and student quality.
