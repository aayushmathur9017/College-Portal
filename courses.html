Our Courses

ABES Engineering College offers industry-driven programs designed to build future-ready professionals with strong academic and practical foundations.

Bachelor of Computer Applications (BCA)

Duration: 3 years | Eligibility: 10+2 (Any stream)

About: Focuses on software development, web technologies, and programming languages to prepare students for IT careers.

  • Programming in C, C++, Java, Python
  • Database & Web Application Development
  • Mini Projects & Industry Internship

Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech)

Duration: 4 years | Eligibility: 10+2 (PCM)

About: Offers specializations in CSE, ECE, Mechanical, Civil & IT. Focused on innovation and real-world problem solving.

  • Data Structures, IoT & Cloud Computing
  • Engineering Design, AI & Machine Learning
  • Major Project in Final Year

Master of Technology (M.Tech)

Duration: 2 years | Eligibility: B.Tech / BE

About: Advanced research-oriented program emphasizing emerging technologies and innovation.

  • AI, Robotics & Machine Learning
  • Research Thesis & Publications
  • Project Collaboration with Industry

Master of Computer Applications (MCA)

Duration: 2 years | Eligibility: BCA / B.Sc (IT)

About: Focuses on software architecture, data science, and project-based learning for tech leadership roles.

  • Full Stack Development
  • AI, Data Analytics, DevOps
  • Capstone Industry Project

Master of Business Administration (MBA)

Duration: 2 years | Eligibility: Bachelor's degree (Any stream)

About: Builds leadership and management skills with a focus on marketing, finance, and HR analytics.

  • Marketing, Finance & HR Specializations
  • Live Case Studies & Internships
  • Corporate Guest Lectures

Master of Science (M.Sc)

Duration: 2 years | Eligibility: B.Sc or equivalent

About: Advanced science program offering Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, and Biology specializations.

  • Research-Based Projects
  • Laboratory & Industrial Training
  • Global Research Opportunities
